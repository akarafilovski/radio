Privacy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not receive any data from you.

What stays on your device

Your favorite stations are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

The browser also keeps a copy of the page files on your device so it opens quickly.

Radio stations

When you play a station, your browser connects directly to that station's own streaming server, and loads station logos from the stations' websites. Those servers see your IP address like any website you visit. The station list comes from the public radio-browser.info directory and our own curated lists.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you load the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and the player for some streams from cdnjs.

Tips

The Support section shows a public Solana address. If you choose to send a tip, the transfer happens in your own wallet app and is recorded on the public Solana blockchain. We do not see anything from the website itself.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
